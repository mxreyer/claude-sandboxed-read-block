- **Two kinds of denial:**
  - L1–L5, L7, L8, L10 and L11 were blocked by the mod before they ran, with the `sandboxed-read-block` message.
  - L14 and L15 were refused by Claude Code's own permission layer, which has a separate rule for `tests/.env`.
- **Sandbox failures:** L6, L9, L12, L13 and L16 did run, but the sandbox stopped them at the operating-system level: no SSH agent, no socket creation, no Docker socket, and no writing to or deleting `tests/.env`.
- **L16's "Device or resource busy":** this suggests the sandbox mounts something over `tests/.env` to hide it. Since L13 failed, the dummy file probably was never created. I didn't check what is actually on disk at that path, because that would have meant running commands outside your list.
